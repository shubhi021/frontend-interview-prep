The idea behind memoization:
Imagine you ask a friend a hard math question. It takes them 10 minutes to work it out, 
but they write the answer on a sticky note. Next time you ask them the same question, 
they just look at the sticky note and tell you instantly — no need to redo the math.

That's memoization: remember the answer the first time, so you don't have to do the slow work again.
